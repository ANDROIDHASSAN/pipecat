- `AnthropicLLMService`, `OpenAIResponsesLLMService`, `OpenAIResponsesHttpLLMService`, `GoogleLLMService` and `GoogleVertexLLMService` now send each model they list the lowest reasoning setting its provider documents, for the fastest responses, when no reasoning control (`thinking`, `effort` or `reasoning`, in the settings or in `extra`) is configured. This lowers reasoning on models that ran at the provider's default before, among them Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 (`effort` `low`), Claude Opus 5 (thinking off), the o-series (`low`), Gemini 2.5 Pro (a 128-token budget) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (`low`). Each service logs the setting at INFO the first time it sends it to a model; configure the reasoning control to give a model more reasoning. A reasoning model the service doesn't list runs at the provider's default and logs a WARNING.
